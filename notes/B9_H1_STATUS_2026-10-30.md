# B9 H1 status — backend finding and local mitigation

Cutoff: 2026-10-30. H1 discovery is stopped; **backend finding and local mitigation are documented, upstream hardening remains open**. Detailed unpublished trigger and mitigation material is excluded from the proposed public update pending disclosure review.

The evidence has separate components: an arithmetic/resource-accounting finding in the backend; a historical host-global-OOM termination during compilation; and two later recorded compute-ring timeout events followed by successful ring resets. The backend defect was not isolated as the cause of those runtime timeouts. No cross-client impact, unauthorized device memory access or security-boundary violation is demonstrated. A ring reset is not automatically a full-device reset.

The 30 October local package documents a narrow RadeonSI/Rusticl mitigation with compile-only regression and program/library-path checks. Its 56-file manifest verifies without mismatch. Those checks used bounded host resources and did not dispatch GPU work. That statement applies to the mitigation checks; it does not erase the earlier runtime events. The mitigation is not a complete ACO-wide fix, and untested driver/device/error paths remain qualified in the local record.

The 17 October report's original no-recovery statement is superseded by its 20 October addendum, and the 30 October mitigation report is authoritative for the local fix scope. Original reports, logs, inputs and patches are retained locally. A public technical report or upstream submission is a separate review step.
