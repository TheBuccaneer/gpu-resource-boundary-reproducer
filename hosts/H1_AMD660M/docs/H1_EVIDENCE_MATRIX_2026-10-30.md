## Per-class evidence and limitations

### B1 — resource capacity

**Hypothesis/oracle:** the dispatch path must account for effective per-workgroup local storage and contain an infeasible request before unsafe execution. The exact 65536-byte control accesses its first/last byte successfully; the requested 65537-byte object is accessed within its requested extent. The specific normative rejection stage/code was not archived as a pinned specification snapshot in the final gate; do not invent one now.

**Method and evidence:** [byte probe](../src/dynamic_local_byte_boundary_probe.c), [exact control](../results/b1/H1_B1_DYN_BYTE_65536_exact_boundary_2026-08-26.txt), [+1 result](../results/b1/H1_B1_DYN_BYTE_65537_boundary_plus1_2026-08-26.txt), [kernel evidence](../results/b1/H1_B1_DYN_BYTE_65537_kernel_evidence_2026-08-26.txt), [instrumented 26.2.0 result](../results/b1/H1_B1_EH5_Mesa26.2_DYN_BYTE_65537_plus1_2026-08-28.txt). Source instrumentation observes 65537 bytes and 129 LDS blocks against the 65536-byte limit. Root-cause evidence supports missing dynamic capacity revalidation; no hardware zero-occupancy assertion. No independent cross-client effect is inferred from these B1 logs. Counts remain per file/campaign; local fix and a version-pinned CTS coverage conclusion are absent.

### B2 — geometry

**Hypothesis/oracle:** kernel-specific workgroup size and dimensions must be enforced at enqueue. [Workgroup probe](../src/b2_workgroup_probe.c), [dimension probe](../src/b2_workdim_probe.c), [required-size probe](../src/b2_reqd_wg_probe.c), and [2D probe](../src/b2_workgroup_2d_probe.c) exercise the cases in the class gate. Expected invalid results include `CL_INVALID_WORK_GROUP_SIZE` and `CL_INVALID_WORK_DIMENSION`; they are observed in the [513 case](../results/b2/H1_B2_C5_WG513_boundary_plus1_2026-08-28.txt), [dimension-4 case](../results/b2/H1_B2_C6b_workdim4_2026-08-28.txt) and named companion records. Device max 1024 and tested kernel max 512 are distinct. Valid 64/256/511/512 and 16×32 cases PASS. These finite controls do not prove every geometry path safe; there is no implementation fault to fix and no established CTS omission.

### B3 — composition/alignment

**Hypothesis/oracle:** two individually admissible local arguments can jointly exceed capacity; raw accounting must not hide aligned dispatch demand. [Probe](../src/b3_multi_local_probe.c), [raw excess](../results/b3/H1_B3_C5_two_locals_65537_combined_plus1_2026-08-28.txt), [alignment-hidden result](../results/b3/H1_B3_C6b_alignment_hidden_plus1_2026-08-28.txt), and [instrumented result](../results/b3/H1_B3_EH5f_instrumented_alignment_hidden_plus1_2026-08-28.txt) distinguish 32768+32769 from 32767+32769. The latter reports raw 65536 while aligned footprint is 65537. Expected containment is contrasted with accepted dispatch, timeout/recovery and abort. Source and instrumented release build support the layout/validation mechanism. No new fix or version-pinned CTS absence claim is present. This is closely related to B1 and should not inflate independent mechanism counts.

### B4 — SVM and later lifetime follow-up

**Original oracle:** event-ordered `clEnqueueSVMFree` host lifetime work must follow completion of the using command. Strong evidence is actual corruption of a fresh allocation lifetime, not address reuse alone. [Callback GPU-overlap source](../src/b4_svm_device_containment_noprofile_probe.c), [NULL-callback source](../src/b4_svm_null_callback_gpu_overlap_probe.c), [causal patch](../patches/b4/rusticl_svmfree_completion_causal.patch), and [baseline/fix evidence](../results/b4/20261004_164007_b4_security_effect_baseline_vs_fix/) establish the original result. The [NULL-callback differential](../results/b4/20261004_173524_null_callback_gpu_overlap_baseline_vs_fix/) has 5/5 affected and 5/5 corrected runs. [Corrected cross-queue control](../results/b4/20261004_165436_cross_queue_mechanism_fixed/) is clean 5/5; runs missing `clFlush(q2)` are discarded.

**Boundary:** original F8 is intra-context cross-lifetime integrity, not cross-process access. The later ordinary-object lifetime follow-up is a separate closed local research record with a narrowly confirmed PoF; detailed sources, patch and archives remain outside the upload pending disclosure review. Its physical memory impact was not demonstrated. The [public follow-up status](../notes/B4_FOLLOWUP_STATUS_2026-10-30.md) records this restriction. No broad CTS absence or all-command fix claim is made.

### B5 — raw pointers

**Admissibility:** the foreign-pointer dereference deliberately lies outside defined extension usage. Functional base/interior/last-word controls establish the harness; the foreign address then yields target markers in 5/5 processes on each tested Mesa version. [Source](../src/b5_cross_context_foreign_read_probe.c), [system repeats](../results/b5/c6b-repro/), and [26.2 repeats](../results/b5/c6b-repro-mesa26.2/) support the empirical result. Context-local resource tracking and a separately preserved numerical address explain the enforcement split. The older report's F7/security wording is qualified by its own undefined-usage caveat and the current project interpretation. No cross-process/cross-user read, formal protection-domain violation, spec error-code requirement, or corrective patch is established.

### B6 and B11 — deferred capability/configuration

The [20 October device inventory](../artifacts/b10_c0_host_clinfo_2026-10-20.txt) supplies extension/device/partition information. Absence of the required mutable-command capability and absence of an independent multi-device/ICD configuration prevent the intended hypotheses. No test outcome, expected API failure, root cause, reproduction count, fix or CTS gap is assigned. Re-evaluation belongs to H2 capability inventory, not more H1 probing.

### B7 — ordinary object ownership

**Oracle:** [Khronos `clSetKernelArg`](https://registry.khronos.org/OpenCL/specs/unified/refpages/man/html/clSetKernelArg.html) requires an object from the kernel's context and specifies `CL_INVALID_CONTEXT` for mismatch. C0 metadata and valid-function controls pass; the wrong object setter succeeds 5/5 and forward foreign modification repeats 5/5, with a reverse-direction control. [Sources](../src/b7_context_ownership_probe.c), [forward source](../src/b7_c1_foreign_mem_dispatch.c), [reverse source](../src/b7_c2_reverse_foreign_mem_dispatch.c), [matched local differential](../results/b7/20261003_082740_mesa262_valid_baseline_vs_b7fix/) and [patch](../patches/b7/rusticl_clSetKernelArg_context_validation.patch) establish the missing context check and bounded fix. The incorrect 08:25 library setup is excluded. Upstream status and a fully pinned CTS coverage assessment remain open; the result requires the foreign object handle inside one host process.

### B8 — representation

**Oracle:** semantically paired Source/IL programs should produce the same correct output/accounting for the selected valid inputs. [Metadata source](../src/b8_c1_source_il_metadata.c), [dynamic-local source](../src/b8_c2_source_il_probe.c), [Source input](../probes/dynamic_local.cl) and [IL input](../artifacts/b8_c2_dynamic_local.spv) are present. The closure reports matching add1 and 4096/65532/65536-byte pairs; 65537-byte metadata is queried but enqueue is blocked. No durable paired stdout was found in `results/` or `artifacts/` during consolidation. Thus the empirical rows are report-backed, not newly raw-log-verified. Mesa 26.2.0 source asymmetry cannot be silently assigned to the 26.0.8 runtime. No bug, fix or CTS-gap claim follows.

### B9 — arithmetic/backend

**Evidence tiers:** the local record separates a backend resource-arithmetic defect, historical host-global-OOM termination, and two later compute-ring recoveries. These are not one proved causal chain. The 30 October mitigation package and its 56-file manifest verify locally; its eight cases, program/library paths and retries were compile-only with bounded host resources. Those checks establish a narrow mitigation, not a complete ACO-wide correction or proof of device-side memory-safety impact. Detailed trigger and patch artifacts remain local for disclosure review; the [public status](../notes/B9_H1_STATUS_2026-10-30.md) retains qualified status. A normative admissibility boundary and pinned CTS scope for extreme resource cases are incomplete. Upstream hardening remains open.

### B10 — synchronization/state

**Oracle/method:** explicit event dependencies, completion and data canaries are checked separately. [C1 source](../src/b10_c1_baseline.c), [cross-queue source](../src/b10_t1_cross_queue.c), [user-event source](../src/b10_t2_user_event.c), [FillBuffer source](../src/b10_final_foreign_event_probe.c), and matching logs in `artifacts/b10_*2026-10-20.log` are selected. Two official CTS tests—`negative_enqueue_marker_with_wait_list` and `event_enqueue_marker`—passed at commit `9feccbb8fcb8eefeebf86a48036173a8155f9d21`; see [T3 report](../notes/B10_T3_CTS_2026-10-20.md). The negative cases return the specified context/waitlist/status errors. The counts are one per mode, not a stress study. CTS logs lack their own library map/journal, and no optional Out-of-Order run occurred. No new defect or mitigation is claimed.

### B12 — external-semaphore metadata

**Oracle:** [Khronos semaphore queries](https://registry.khronos.org/OpenCL/specs/unified/refpages/man/html/clGetSemaphoreInfoKHR.html) require the object-specific export-handle list. Successfully configuring Sync-FD export predicts one element (`0x2058`, four bytes). The [unchanged source](../src/b12_c1_t1_2026-10-30.c), [C1 log](../artifacts/b12/b12_c1_t1_2026-10-30.log) and [R1 log](../artifacts/b12/b12_r1_t1_2026-10-30.log) show 2/2 empty lists at success despite exportable=true; both releases succeed. System ICD SHA-256 `8cc0f8a269a310e3e48527c0e029ab84f4d7a27144cd9261a78a478638f7870d` and process maps exclude the B9 build. The matching Ubuntu 26.0.8 source hardcodes `None` for this query; its serializer reports zero bytes. No corrected runtime was tested. The CTS gap is limited to the named commit and examined `cl_khr_semaphore` / `cl_khr_external_semaphore` directories. This is RQ1/RQ8, F1, no demonstrated security-boundary violation. External memory is historically unavailable; GL-sharing prerequisites are unproved; neither path was exercised.

## Supplementary campaigns and paper interpretation

[SEC-1](../notes/H1_SEC1_CROSS_CLIENT_CONTAINMENT_CLOSED.md) supplies independent-process availability evidence with canary and GUI-less controls; it must not be conflated with B9's unproved cross-client impact. [SEC-2](../notes/H1_SEC2_LDS_ISOLATION_CLOSED.md) is a residual-state characterization using intentionally uninitialized reads. [O2](../notes/H1_O2_POST_RESET_LDS_CLOSED.md) is sensitivity-limited and cannot establish reset sanitization.

B1/B3 and original B4 offer mechanism/causal-control depth; B7 offers a clean object-contract comparison; B12 offers a deterministic Host-API conformance case. B8/B10 and deferred capabilities make the selection method transparent. Cross-vendor generality is untested. The additional lifetime follow-up may strengthen the mechanism study after disclosure review; it must not be counted as a new demonstrated corruption effect. The paper should report families of mechanisms and bounded observations, not equate the number of B classes with independent vulnerabilities.
